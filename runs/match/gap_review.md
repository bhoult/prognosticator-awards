# Gap concepts (3c): review before milestone research

11,923 claims were coded no_concept after both passes. Agents proposed concepts for what they kept returning to; one agent per domain group consolidated them against the approved registry, and one more merged duplicates across groups. The gap pass then matched 4,712 of the 11,923 (40%).

- **New concepts:** 739 (69 with fewer than 3 matched claims).
- **Where a cut would fall:** keeping those with at least 3 matched claims keeps 670 concepts and 4,602 claims; keeping those with at least 5 matched claims keeps 444 concepts and 3,801 claims; keeping those with at least 10 matched claims keeps 132 concepts and 1,727 claims.
- **Proposals that duplicated an approved concept:** 37, for 37 concepts (claims the earlier passes missed).
- **Codes after the gap pass:** {'too_vague': 479, 'no_concept': 6676, 'untestable': 56}.

Each new concept will get milestone research. To drop or merge one, add a row to `runs/match/concept_review.jsonl` before research starts.
